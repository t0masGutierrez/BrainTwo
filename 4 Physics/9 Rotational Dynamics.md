### torque
- rotational analogue of force
![[4 Physics/Images/torque.png|300]]

---
### torque formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F=(\|\vec r\|)(\|\vec F\|)\sin(\phi)\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
(\|\vec r\|),(\|\vec F\|)=\text{magnitude}\\
\phi=\text{direction}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### tangential torque
- component of force perpendicular radius
![[4 Physics/Images/tangential torque.png|300]]

---
### tangential torque formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau=rF_{\perp}\\
r=\text{distance}\\
F_{\perp}=\text{perpendicular force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### lever torque
- perpendicular distance from the axis of rotation to the line of action of the force
![[4 Physics/Images/lever torque.png|300]]

---
### lever torque formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau=r_{\perp}F\\
r=\text{perpendicular distance}\\
F=\text{force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### torque direction property
- counterclockwise rotation equal positive torque
- if line of action equal axis of rotation then zero torque
- clockwise rotation equal negative torque
![[4 Physics/Images/torque direction property.png|300]]

---
### torque direction property formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\circlearrowleft\implies\tau>0\\
\hat r\parallel\hat F\implies\tau=0\\
\circlearrowright\implies\tau<0
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons second law of rotation
- angular acceleration dependent moment of inertia and net torque acting on moment of inertia
![[4 Physics/Images/newtons second law.png]]

---
### newtons second law of rotation formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec\tau_{\text{net}}=I\vec\alpha\\
I=\text{moment of inertia}\\
\vec\alpha=\text{angular acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rotational work
- transfer of mechanical energy by the component of torque parallel angular displacement
![[4 Physics/Images/rotational work.png|200]]

---
### rotational work formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
W=\int\vec\tau\cdot d\vec\theta\\
\vec\tau=\text{torque}\\
\vec\theta=\text{angular position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rotational power
- rate of doing rotational work

---
### rotational power formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
P=\vec\tau\cdot\vec\omega=(\|\vec\tau\|)(\|\vec\omega\|)\cos(\phi)\\
\vec\tau=\text{torque}\\
\vec\omega=\text{angular velocity}\\
\|\vec\tau\|,\|\vec\omega\|=\text{magnitude}\\
\phi=\text{direction}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rolling kinetic energy
- translational kinetic energy and rotational kinetic energy
![[4 Physics/Images/rolling kinetic energy.png|300]]

---
### rolling kinetic energy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
K=\frac{1}{2}Mv_{\text{cm}}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}I_{\text{cm}}\omega^{2}\\
M=\text{system mass}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
I=\text{moment of inertia}\\
\omega=\text{angular velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rolling without slipping
- stationary point of contact between rolling object and surface
- static friction force
![[4 Physics/Images/rolling without slipping.png|400]]

---
### rolling without slipping formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{\text{cm}}=-R\theta\\
v_{\text{cm}}=-R\omega\\
a_{\text{cm}}=-R\alpha\\
R=\text{radius}\\
\theta=\text{angular position}\\
\omega=\text{angular velocity}\\
\alpha=\text{angular acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rolling with slipping
- moving point of contact between rolling object and surface
- kinetic friction force

---
### rolling with slipping formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x=x_{\text{cm}}+R\theta\\
v=v_{\text{cm}}+R\omega\\
a=a_{\text{cm}}+R\alpha\\
x=\text{position}\\
R=\text{radius}\\
\theta=\text{angular position}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
\omega=\text{angular velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}\\
\alpha=\text{angular acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### angular momentum
- difficulty of stopping or changing direction of rotational motion
![[4 Physics/Images/angular momentum.png|300]]

---
### angular momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec L=\vec r\times\vec p=I\vec\omega\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}\\
I=\text{moment of inertia}\\
\vec\omega=\text{angular velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### angular momentum direction property
- direction of angular velocity equal direction of angular momentum
![[4 Physics/Images/angular momentum direction property.png|200]]

---
### angular momentum direction property formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat\omega=\hat L\\
\omega=\text{angular velocity}\\
L=\text{angular momentum}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons second law of angular momentum
- net torque equal rate of change of angular momentum

---
### newtons second law of angular momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec\tau_{\text{net}}=\frac{d\vec L}{dt}\\
\vec L=\text{angular momentum}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### angular impulse
- change of angular momentum equal sum of net torque over time
![[4 Physics/Images/impulse.png|300]]

---
### angular impulse formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec H=\Delta\vec L=\int\vec\tau_{\text{net}}\cdot dt\\
\vec L=\text{angular momentum}\\
\vec\tau=\text{torque}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### conservation of angular momentum
- if zero net torque then zero change of angular momentum

---
### conservation of angular momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec\tau_{\text{net}}=0\implies\vec H=0\\
\vec\tau=\text{torque}\\
\vec H=\text{angular impulse}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### precession
- circular motion of flywheel axis and rotational motion of flywheel
![[4 Physics/Images/precession.png|200]]

---
### precession formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Omega=\frac{mgr}{I\omega}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
g=\text{gravity}\\
r=\text{distance}\\
I=\text{moment of inertia}\\
\omega=\text{angular velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### static equilibrium
- object at rest

---
### static equilibrium formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v=0\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### dynamic equilibrium
- object in motion with constant velocity

---
### dynamic equilibrium formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d\vec v}{dt}=0\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### translational equilibrium
- zero net force

---
### translational equilibrium formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=0\\
\vec F=\text{force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rotational equilibrium
- zero net torque

---
### rotational equilibrium formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec\tau_{\text{net}}=0\\
\vec\tau=\text{torque}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### center of gravity
- point inside object acting as the location of total weight
![[4 Physics/Images/center of gravity.png|300]]

---
### center of gravity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec r_{\text{cg}}=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{n}m_{n}\vec r_{n}\\
M=\text{system mass}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec r=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### center of mass
- if uniform gravitational field then center of gravity equal center of mass
![[4 Physics/Images/uniform center of gravity.png|200]]

---
### center of mass formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec g(\vec r)=\vec g_{0}\implies\vec r_{\text{cg}}=\vec r_{\text{cm}}\\
\vec g=\text{gravity}\\
\vec r=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### area of support
- region between point of contact
![[4 Physics/Images/area of support.png|400]]

---
### area of support formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec r_{\text{cg}}\not\in A\implies\downarrow\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### stress
- force per cross-section area
- tensile/compressive, bulk, shear

---
### stress formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{Y}=\frac{F}{A}\\
\sigma_{B}=\Delta p\\
\sigma_{S}=\frac{F_{\parallel}}{A}\\
F=\text{force}\\
A=\text{area}\\
p=\text{pressure}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### strain
- deformation of material

---
### strain formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\epsilon_{Y}=\frac{\Delta L}{L_{0}}\\
\epsilon_{B}=\frac{\Delta V}{V_{0}}\\
\epsilon_{S}=\frac{\Delta x}{h}\\
L=\text{length}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
x=\text{position}\\
h=\text{height}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### elastic modulus
- stiffness of material
![[4 Physics/Images/elastic modulus.png|500]]

---
### elastic modulus formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
Y=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\\
B=\frac{-\Delta p}{\Delta V/V_{0}}\\
S=\frac{F_{\parallel}/A}{\Delta x/h}\\
\sigma=\text{stress}\\
\epsilon=\text{strain}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
F=\text{force}\\
A=\text{area}\\
x=\text{position}\\
h=\text{height}
\end{aligned}
$$
