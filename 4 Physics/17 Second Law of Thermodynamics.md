### irreversible process
- change of state such that the system and surroundings cannot return initial state with zero net change

---
### irreversible process example
- finite temperature difference
- finite pressure difference
- finite concentration difference
- friction, viscosity, or resistance

---
### reversible process
- quasi-static change of state such that the system and surroundings can return initial state with zero net change

---
### reversible process example
- infinitesimal temperature difference
- infinitesimal pressure difference
- infinitesimal concentration difference
- zero dissipation

---
### heat engine
- transform heat into work
- transform thermal energy into mechanical energy
![[4 Physics/Images/heat engine.png]]

---
### heat engine formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
W=Q_H-Q_C\\
\eta=\frac{W}{Q_{H}}\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
W=\text{work}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### internal combustion engine
- piston moves down, intake valve opens, and air-fuel mixture enters the cylinder
- piston moves up, intake valve closes, and piston compresses the mixture
- spark plug ignites the compressed mixture
- hot gases expand and push the piston down thus producing work
- piston moves up, exhaust valve opens, and burned gas exits the cylinder
![](4%20Physics/Images/four%20stroke%20internal%20combustion%20engine.png)

---
### internal combustion engine example
- gasoline engine
- diesel engine

---
### thermodynamic cycle
- thermodynamic process whose final state equal initial state
- clockwise cycle equal positive work
- counterclockwise cycle equal negative work
![[4 Physics/Images/thermodynamic cycle.png]]

---
### thermodynamic cycle formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=W\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
W=\text{work}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### otto cycle
- thermodynamic cycle of internal combustion gasoline engine
![300](4%20Physics/Images/otto%20cycle.png)

---
### otto cycle formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta=1-\frac{1}{(\frac{V_a}{V_b})^{\gamma-1}}\\
\gamma=\frac{C_{p}}{C_{V}}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
C=\text{molar heat capacity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### diesel cycle
- thermodynamic cycle of internal combustion diesel engine
![300](4%20Physics/Images/diesel%20cycle.png)

---
### diesel cycle formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta=1-\frac{\rho^{\gamma-1}-1}{\gamma(\rho-1)(\frac{V_a}{V_b})^{\gamma-1}}\\
\rho=\frac{V_c}{V_b}\\
\gamma=\frac{C_{p}}{C_{V}}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
C=\text{molar heat capacity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### refrigerator
- subtract heat from cold region
- aka reverse heat engine
![](4%20Physics/Images/refrigerator.png)

---
### refrigerator formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{COP}_{\text{R}}=\frac{Q_C}{W}=\frac{Q_{C}}{Q_{H}-Q_{C}}\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
W=\text{work}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### heat pump
- add heat to hot region
- aka reverse heat engine
![200](4%20Physics/Images/heat%20pump.png)

---
### heat pump formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{COP}_\text{HP}=\frac{Q_H}{W}=\frac{Q_{H}}{Q_{H}-Q_{C}}\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
W=\text{work}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### second law of thermodynamics
- heat flow from high temperature to low temperature until thermal equilibrium
- fluid flow from high pressure to low pressure until mechanical equilibrium
- chemical flow from high concentration to low concentration until chemical equilibrium
![300](4%20Physics/Images/second%20law%20of%20thermodynamics.png)

---
### second law of thermodynamics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{A}<T_{B}\implies T_{A}+Q=T_{B}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
Q=\text{heat}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### carnot cycle
- thermodynamic cycle operating at maximum efficiency between hot reservoir and cold reservoir
![400](4%20Physics/Images/carnot%20cycle.png)

---
### carnot cycle formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta=\frac{T_{H}-T_{C}}{T_{H}}=\frac{Q_{H}-Q_{C}}{Q_{H}}\\
\text{COP}_{\text{R}}=\frac{Q_C}{W}=\frac{T_{C}}{T_{H}-T_{C}}\\
\text{COP}_{\text{HP}}=\frac{Q_H}{W}=\frac{T_{H}}{T_{H}-T_{C}}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
W=\text{work}
\end{aligned}
$$

### entropy
- number of possible microstates corresponding with macrostate
- or quantitative measure of randomness
![200](4%20Physics/Images/entropy.png)

---
### entropy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
S=k\ln(\Omega)\\
\Delta S=\int\frac{dQ}{T}\\
k_{b}=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
\Omega=\text{number of microstates}\\
Q=\text{heat}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### second law of thermodynamics
- universal entropy of isolated system can increase but cannot decrease

---
### second law of thermodynamics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta S=\Delta S_{\text{system}}+\Delta S_{\text{surroundings}}\ge0\\
S=\text{entropy}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### third law of thermodynamics
- entropy decreases as temperature decreases

---
### third law of thermodynamics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
S=k\ln(1)=0\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}
\end{aligned}
$$
