### dynamics
- study of how forces affect the motion of objects

---
### dynamics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{A}\rightarrow\vec x_{A}=?
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### force
- vector quantity of push or pull on object
![[4 Physics/Images/force.png|300]]

---
### force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F=F_{x}\hat i+F_{y}\hat j\\
F=\sqrt{F_{x}^{2}+F_{y}^{2}}\\
\theta=\arctan(\frac{F_{y}}{F_{x}})\\
F_{x}=F\cos(\theta)\\
F_{y}=F\sin(\theta)\\
F=\text{force}\\
\hat i=\text{x direction}\\
\hat j=\text{y direction}\\
\theta=\text{angle between vectors}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### net force
- vector sum of all forces acting on object
![[4 Physics/Images/net force.png|300]]

---
### net force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=\sum_{n}\vec F_{n}=(\sum_{n}F_{\text{nx}})\hat i+(\sum_{n}F_{\text{ny}})\hat j\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
\hat i=\text{x direction}\\
\hat j=\text{y direction}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### normal force
- perpendicular force by surface on object
![[4 Physics/Images/normal force.png|300]]

---
### normal force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{N}=m\vec a_{N}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### friction force
- resistive force by surface on object
- static friction acts on stationary objects
- kinetic friction acts on sliding objects
![[4 Physics/Images/friction force.png|300]]

---
### friction force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec f_{s}\le\mu_{s}\vec F_{N}\\
\vec f_{k}=\mu_{k}\vec F_{N}\\
\mu_{s}=\text{static friction coefficient}\\
\mu_{k}=\text{kinetic friction coefficient}\\
\vec F=\text{force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### tension force

![[4 Physics/Images/tension force.png|300]]

---
### tension force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{T}=m\vec a_{T}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### weight force
- downward force by gravity on object
![[4 Physics/Images/weight force.png|300]]

---
### weight force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec w=m\vec g\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec g=\text{gravity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### centripetal force
- center seeking force causing uniform circular motion
![[4 Physics/Images/centripetal force.png|200]]

---
### centripetal force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{c}=m\vec a_{\text{rad}}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### spring force
- restoring force by spring on object
![[4 Physics/Images/spring force.png|300]]

---
### spring force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{s}=-k\vec x\\
k=\text{spring constant}\\
\vec x=\text{displacement}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### drag force
- resistive force by fluid on object

---
### drag force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{d}=-k\vec v\\
\vec F_{d}=\frac{-1}{2}\rho_fC_{d}A\vec v^{2}\\
k=\text{linear drag coefficient}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\rho_f=\text{fluid density}\\
C_{d}=\text{drag coefficient}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### buoyant force
- upward force by fluid on immersed object

---
### buoyant force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{b}=\rho_{f}V_{f}\vec g\\
\rho_{f}=\text{fluid density}\\
V_{f}=\text{fluid volume}\\
\vec g=\text{gravity}
\end{aligned}
$$

### free body diagram
- draw body as the origin of the coordinate system
- sketch all forces acting on the body as vectors
- resolve all vectors into scalar components
- if equilibrium then newtons first law $\sum F=0$
- if nonequilibrium then newtons second law $\sum F=ma$
![[4 Physics/Images/free body diagram.png|200]]

### gravitational interaction
- responsible for the attraction between masses

---
### gravitational interaction formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{G}=G\frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{r^{2}}\hat r\\
G=6.67\times10^{-11}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
r=\text{distance}\\
\hat r=\text{direction}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### electromagnetic interaction
- responsible for the attraction/repulsion between electric charges

---
### electromagnetic interaction formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{EM}}=q(\vec E+\vec v\times\vec B)\\
q=\text{electric charge}\\
\vec E=\text{electric field}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\vec B=\text{magnetic field}
\end{aligned}
$$

### newtons first law
- object at rest remains at rest or moves with constant velocity unless acted on by nonzero net external force
![[4 Physics/Images/newtons first law.png]]

---
### newtons 1st formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=0\implies\vec a=0\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons second law
- acceleration dependent mass and net external force acting on mass
![[4 Physics/Images/newtons second law.png]]

---
### newtons 2nd formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=m\vec a=
\begin{cases}
F_{x}=ma_{x}\\
F_{y}=ma_{y}
\end{cases}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}\\
F=\text{force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons third law
- for every action force there exists equal and opposite reaction force
- equal magnitude and opposite direction
![[4 Physics/Images/newtons third law.png|200]]

---
### newtons 3rd formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{AB}}=-\vec F_{\text{BA}}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
A,B=\text{object}
\end{aligned}
$$
