### molar mass
- amount of mass per mole

---
### molar mass formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\frac{m}{n}=N_Am\\
m=\text{mass}\\
n=\text{number of moles}\\
N_A=6.022\times10^{23}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### avogadros number
- number of particles per mole

---
### avogadros number formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{A}=\frac{N}{n}=6.022\times10^{23}\\
N=\text{number of particles}\\
n=\text{number of moles}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### ideal gas
- gas particles are point particles with negligible volume
- gas particles move randomly with constant velocity
- gas particles undergo elastic collisions
![[4 Physics/Images/ideal gas.png]]

---
### ideal gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
pV=NkT=nRT=nkN_{A}T\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
N=\text{number of particles}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
n=\text{number of moles}\\
R=8.314\\
N_{A}=6.022\times10^{23}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### van der waals gas
- gas particles have finite volume
- gas particles attract each other
![300](4%20Physics/Images/van%20der%20waals%20gas.png)

---
### van der waals gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
(p+\frac{an^2}{V^2})(V-nb)=NkT\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
a=\text{intermolecular attraction}\\
n=\text{number of moles}\\
b=\text{molecular volume}\\
N=\text{number of particles}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### boyles gas
- if constant temperature closed system then pressure inversely proportional volume
- isothermal
![[4 Physics/Images/boyles gas.png]]

---
### boyles gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{1}V_{1}=p_{2}V_{2}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
V=\text{volume}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### charles gas
- if constant pressure closed system then volume directly proportional temperature
- isobaric
![[4 Physics/Images/charles gas.png]]

---
### charles gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{V_{1}}{T_{1}}=\frac{V_{2}}{T_{2}}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### gay-lussacs gas
- if constant volume then pressure directly proportional temperature
- isochoric
![300](4%20Physics/Images/gay-lussacs%20gas.png)

---
### gay-lussacs gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{p_{1}}{T_{1}}=\frac{p_{2}}{T_{2}}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### combined gas
- if constant number of moles then energy directly proportional temperature

---
### combined gas formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{p_1V_{1}}{T_{1}}=\frac{p_2V_{2}}{T_{2}}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### translational kinetic energy
- internal energy of ideal monatomic gas

---
### translational kinetic energy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
K=\frac{3}{2}NkT\\
N=\text{number of particles}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### average translational kinetic energy
- translational kinetic energy per particle
![[4 Physics/Images/average translational kinetic energy.png|300]]

---
### average translational kinetic energy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
K_{\text{avg}}=\frac{3}{2}kT=\frac{1}{2}mv_{\text{rms}}^2\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### velocity
- nonuniform probability density function of randomly moving gas particle
![[4 Physics/Images/velocity.png|300]]

---
### velocity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(v)=\frac{4}{\sqrt\pi{}}(\frac{m}{2kT})^{\frac{3}{2}}v^{2}\exp{(\frac{-mv^{2}}{2kT})}\\
v_{\text{max}}=\sqrt{\frac{2kT}{m}}\\
v_{\text{rms}}=\sqrt{\frac{3kT}{m}}\\
v_{\text{avg}}=\sqrt{\frac{8kT}{\pi m}}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### mean free path
- average distance traveled between collision
![300](4%20Physics/Images/mean%20free%20path.png)

---
### mean free path formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda=\frac{V}{4\pi r^2N\sqrt2}=\frac{kT}{4\pi r^2p\sqrt2}\\
V=\text{volume}\\
r=\text{radius}\\
N=\text{number of particles}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}\\
p=\text{pressure}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### mean free time
- average time elapsed between collision

---
### mean free time formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau=\frac{\lambda}{v_{\text{rms}}}\\
\lambda=\text{mean free path}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### degrees of freedom
- number of velocity components that describe molecular motion
![500](4%20Physics/Images/degrees%20of%20freedom.png)

---
### degrees of freedom formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{\text{mon}}=3_{\text{transl}}\\
f_{\text{di}}=3_{\text{transl}}+2_{\text{rot}}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### equipartition of energy
- equal partition of internal energy across the degrees of freedom such that for every atom there exists average kinetic energy of $\frac{1}{2}kT$

---
### equipartition of energy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
K_{\text{avg}}=\frac{f}{2}kT\\
f=\text{degrees of freedom}\\
k=1.381\times10^{-23}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### specific heat
- amount of heat required to change the temperature of 1 kilogram of substance by 1 degree celsius without changing its phase
![300](4%20Physics/Images/specific%20heat.png)

---
### specific heat formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=nC\Delta T\\
n=\text{number of moles}\\
C=\text{molar heat capacity}\\
T=\text{temperature}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### molar heat capacity
- high molar heat capacity resist temperature change
![300](4%20Physics/Images/molar%20heat%20capacity1.png)

---
### molar heat capacity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\text{gas}}=\frac{f}{2}R\\
C_{\text{solid}}=3R\\
f=\text{degrees of freedom}\\
R=8.314
\end{aligned}
$$
