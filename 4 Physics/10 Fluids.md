### fluid
- liquid or gas

---
### solid
- definite shape
- definite volume
- vibrational motion
![[4 Physics/Images/solid.png]]

---
### liquid
- indefinite shape
- definite volume
- incompressible fluid
- translational motion
![[4 Physics/Images/liquid.png]]

---
### gas
- indefinite shape
- indefinite volume
- compressible fluid
- random motion
![[4 Physics/Images/gas.png]]

---
### volume
- amount of three dimensional space within object
![[4 Physics/Images/volume.png|500]]

---
### volume formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text{cube}=lwh\\
\text{sphere}=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}\\
\text{cyclinder}=\pi r^{2}h
\end{array}
$$

---
### density
- measure of compactness
![[4 Physics/Images/density.png|400]]

---
### density formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\rho=\frac{m}{V}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
V=\text{volume}
\end{array}
$$

---
### relative density
- measure of compactness with respect to water

---
### relative density formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\rho'=\frac{\rho}{\rho_{\text{water}}}\\
\rho=\text{density}
\end{array}
$$

---
### pressure
- measure of local force
![[4 Physics/Images/pressure.png]]

---
### pressure formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
p=\frac{F_{\perp}}{A}\\
F=\text{force}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{array}
$$

---
### atmospheric pressure
- weight of air column
![[4 Physics/Images/atmospheric pressure.png]]

---
### atmospheric pressure formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text{1 atm}=\text{101325 Pa}
\end{array}
$$

---
### hydrostatic pressure
- weight of fluid column at rest with uniform density
![[4 Physics/Images/hydrostatic pressure.png|250]]

---
### hydrostatic pressure formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\Delta p=-\rho g\Delta y\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
\rho=\text{density}\\
g=\text{gravity}\\
y=\text{position}
\end{array}
$$

---
### absolute pressure
- measure of pressure relative vacuum

---
### absolute pressure formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
p_{\text{abs}}=p_{\text{gauge}}+p_{\text{atm}}=\rho gy+p_{\text{atm}}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
\rho=\text{density}\\
g=\text{gravity}\\
y=\text{position}
\end{array}
$$

---
### gauge pressure
- measure of pressure relative atmospheric pressure

---
### gauge pressure formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
p_{\text{gauge}}=p_{\text{abs}}-p_{\text{atm}}=\rho gy\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
\rho=\text{density}\\
g=\text{gravity}\\
y=\text{position}
\end{array}
$$

### pascals principle
- change of pressure within enclosed fluid transmit equally throughout the fluid
![[4 Physics/Images/pascals principle.png|300]]

---
### pascals principle formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\Delta p_1=\Delta p_2\\
p=\text{pressure}
\end{array}
$$

---
### archimedes principle
- buoyant force equal the weight of displaced fluid
![[4 Physics/Images/archimedes principle.png|400]]

---
### archimedes principle formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec F_{b}=\vec w_{f}\\
\vec w_f=\text{fluid weight}
\end{array}
$$

---
### apparent weight
- immersed object appear less heavy because of buoyant force

---
### apparent weight formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec w_{\text{app}}=\vec w-\vec F_{b}\\
\vec w=\text{weight}\\
\vec F=\text{force}
\end{array}
$$

---
### buoyant force
- upward force by fluid on immersed object because of the pressure imbalance between the top and bottom of immersed object
![[4 Physics/Images/buoyant force.png]]

---
### buoyant force formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec F_{b}=\rho_{f}V_{f}\vec g\\
\rho_{f}=\text{fluid density}\\
V_{f}=\text{fluid volume}\\
\vec g=\text{gravity}
\end{array}
$$

---
### buoyant force direction property
- if buoyant force greater object weight then floating object
- if buoyant force lesser object weight then sinking object
- if buoyant force equal object weight then suspended object

---
### buoyant force direction property formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec F_{b}>\vec w\implies\vec a>0\\
\vec F_{b}<\vec w\implies\vec a<0\\
\vec F_{b}=\vec w\implies\vec a=0
\end{array}
$$

---
### submergence
- fraction of immersed object
![[4 Physics/Images/submergence.png|400]]

---
### submergence formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
S=\frac{\rho}{\rho_{f}}\\
\rho=\text{density}\\
\rho_{f}=\text{fluid density}
\end{array}
$$

---
### submergence direction property
- if fluid density greater object density then floating object
- if fluid density lesser object density then sinking object
- if fluid density equal object density then suspended object

---
### submergence direction property formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\rho_{f}>\rho\implies\uparrow\\
\rho_{f}<\rho\implies\downarrow\\
\rho_{f}=\rho\implies\leftrightarrow
\end{array}
$$

---
### surface tension
- reducing surface area reduces number of high-energy surface molecules
- minimizing surface area minimizes potential energy
![[4 Physics/Images/surface tension.png|200]]

---
### surface tension formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\gamma=\frac{F}{L}=\frac{\Delta E}{\Delta A}\\
F=\text{force}\\
L=\text{length}\\
E=\text{mechanical energy}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{array}
$$

### fluid flow
- motion of fluid

---
### fluid flow formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec v(\vec r,t)\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{array}
$$

### unsteady flow

### ideal fluid
- constant density and zero viscosity

---
### ideal fluid formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\rho=C\\
\mu=0
\end{array}
$$

---
### volume flow rate
- amount of fluid flow through cross-section over time
![[4 Physics/Images/volume flow rate.png|200]]

---
### volume flow rate formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{dV}{dt}=Av\\
V=\text{volume}\\
t=\text{time}\\
A=\text{area}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{array}
$$

---
### continuity
- for steady flow of incompressible fluid there exists inverse relationship between velocity and area
![[4 Physics/Images/continuity.png|400]]

---
### continuity formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
A_{1}v_{1}=A_{2}v_{2}\\
A=\text{area}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{array}
$$

---
### bernoulli principle
- for steady flow of ideal fluid there exists inverse relationship between pressure and velocity
![[4 Physics/Images/bernoulli principle.png|200]]

---
### bernoulli principle formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
p_{1}+\frac{1}{2}\rho v_{1}^{2}+\rho gy_{1}=p_{2}+\frac{1}{2}\rho v_{2}^{2}+\rho gy_{2}\\
p=\text{pressure}\\
\rho=\text{density}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
g=\text{gravity}\\
y=\text{position}
\end{array}
$$

---
### drag force
- resistive force by fluid on object

---
### drag force formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec F_{d}=-k\vec v\\
\vec F_{d}=\frac{-1}{2}\rho_fC_{d}A\vec v^{2}\\
k=\text{linear drag coefficient}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\rho_f=\text{fluid density}\\
C_{d}=\text{drag coefficient}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{array}
$$

---
### terminal velocity
- free falling object with constant velocity

---
### terminal velocity formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\vec w=\vec F_{d}\implies\vec v=\sqrt{\frac{2\vec w}{\rho_fC_{d}A}}\\
\vec w=\text{weight}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\rho_f=\text{fluid density}\\
C_{d}=\text{drag coefficient}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{array}
$$
