### position
- location as function of time

---
### position formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x=x(t)\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### distance
- scalar length of path
![[4 Physics/Images/distance.png|300]]

---
### distance formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
d=\sum_{n}|\Delta x_{n}|\\
x=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### displacement
- vector change of position
![[4 Physics/Images/displacement.png|300]]

---
### displacement formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x=x-x_{0}=\int v\cdot dt\\
x=\text{position}\\
t=\text{time}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### average speed
- distance per time

---
### average speed formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
s_{\text{avg}}=\frac{d}{\Delta t}\\
d=\text{distance}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### instantaneous speed
- magnitude of instantaneous velocity

---
### instantaneous speed formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
s=|v|\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### average velocity
- displacement per time

---
### average velocity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}\\
x=\text{position}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### instantaneous velocity
- rate of change of position as change of time approaches zero
![[4 Physics/Images/instantaneous velocity.png|300]]

---
### instantaneous velocity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v=\frac{dx}{dt}=v_{0}+\int a\cdot dt\\
x=\text{position}\\
t=\text{time}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### average acceleration
- velocity per time

---
### average acceleration formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{\text{avg}}=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### instantaneous acceleration
- rate of change of velocity as change of time approaches zero
![[4 Physics/Images/instantaneous acceleration.png|300]]

---
### instantaneous acceleration formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
t=\text{time}\\
x=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### delta speed property
- if direction of velocity equal direction of acceleration then increasing speed
- if direction of velocity not equal direction of acceleration then decreasing speed
![[4 Physics/Images/changing speed.png|300]]

---
### delta speed property formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat v=\hat a\implies\frac{ds}{dt}>0\\
\hat v\ne\hat a\implies\frac{ds}{dt}<0\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}\\
s=\text{speed}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### kinematics
- describe motion without explaining the cause of motion

---
### kinematics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x,x_{0},v,v_{0},a,t=\ ?
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### first law of motion
- relate velocity, initial velocity, constant acceleration, and time

---
### first law of motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v=v_{0}+at\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### second law of motion
- relate position, initial position, initial velocity, constant acceleration, and time

---
### second law of motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x=x_{0}+v_{0}t+\frac{1}{2}at^{2}\\
x=\text{position}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
t=\text{time}\\
a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### third law of motion
- relate position, initial position, velocity, initial velocity, and time

---
### third law of motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x-x_{0}=\frac{1}{2}(v+v_{0})t\\
x=\text{position}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### fourth law of motion
- relate velocity, initial velocity, constant acceleration, position, and initial position

---
### fourth law of motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v^{2}=v_{0}^{2}+2a(x-x_{0})\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}\\
x=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### free fall
- one-dimensional motion under no other force except gravity
![[4 Physics/Images/free fall.png]]

---
### free fall formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v=v_{0}-gt\\
y=y_{0}+v_{0}t-\frac{1}{2}gt^{2}\\
y-y_{0}=\frac{1}{2}(v+v_{0})t\\
v^{2}=v_{0}^{2}-2g(y-y_{0})
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### relative motion
- motion of object A relative to object B
![[4 Physics/Images/relative motion.png|300]]

---
### relative motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{A/B}=x_{A}-x_{B}\\
v_{A/B}=v_{A}-v_{B}\\
a_{A/B}=a_{A}-a_{B}\\
x=\text{position}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
a=\text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$
