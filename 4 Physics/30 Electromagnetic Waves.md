### wave position
- field magnitude as function of space and time
![[4 Physics/Images/electromagnetic wave position.png|300]]

---
### wave position formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec E(x,t)=E_{0}\cos(kx\mp\omega t)\ \hat j\\
\vec B(x,t)=B_{0}\cos(kx\mp\omega t)\ \hat k\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
k=\text{wave number}\\
x=\text{position}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{lgathered}
$$

![[4 Physics/Images/electromagnetic ratio.png|500]]

---
### electromagnetic ratio formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
c=\frac{E}{B}\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### wave velocity
- rate of wavefront
![[4 Physics/Images/wave velocity.png|300]]

---
### wave velocity formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
v=\lambda f=\frac{\omega}{k}\\
c=\sqrt{\frac{1}{\epsilon_{0}\mu_{0}}}=3\times10^{8}\\
\lambda=\text{wavelength}\\
f=\text{oscillation frequency}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
k=\text{wave number}\\
\epsilon_{0}=8.85\times10^{-12}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}\\
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### non vacuum wave velocity
- rate of wavefront through matter

---
### non vacuum wave velocity formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
v=\sqrt{\frac{1}{\epsilon\mu}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{K\epsilon_{0}K_{m}\mu_{0}}}=c\sqrt{\frac{1}{KK_{m}}}\\
\epsilon=\text{permittivity}\\
\mu=\text{permeability}\\
K=\text{dielectric constant}\\
\epsilon_{0}=8.85\times10^{-12}\\
K_{m}=\text{relative permeability}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}\\
c=3\times10^{8}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### energy density
- measure of electromagnetic energy compactness
![[4 Physics/Images/energy density.png|300]]

---
### average energy density formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
u_{\text{avg}}=\frac{\epsilon_{0}E_{0}^{2}}{2}=\frac{B_{0}^{2}}{2\mu_{0}}\\
\epsilon_{0}=8.85\times10^{-12}\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### instantaneous energy density formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
u=\epsilon_{0}E^{2}=\frac{B^{2}}{\mu_{0}}\\
\epsilon_{0}=8.85\times10^{-12}\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### poynting
- rate of energy flow through normal surface
![[4 Physics/Images/intensity.png]]

---
### average poynting formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
S_{\text{avg}}=c(\frac{\epsilon_{0}E_{0}^{2}}{2})=c(\frac{B_{0}^{2}}{2\mu_{0}})\\
c=3\times10^{8}\\
u=\text{energy density}\\
\epsilon_{0}=8.85\times10^{-12}\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### instantaneous poynting formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec S=\frac{\vec E\times\vec B}{\mu_{0}}\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
\mu_{0}=4\pi\times10^{-7}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### power
- rate of energy transfer

---
### power formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
P=\frac{U}{t}=IA\\
U=\text{energy}\\
t=\text{time}\\
I=\text{intensity}\\
A=\text{area}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### intensity
- magnitude of electromagnetic radiation

---
### intensity formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
I=\frac{P}{A}=S_{\text{avg}}\\
P=\text{power}\\
A=\text{area}\\
S=\text{poynting}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### radiation pressure
- transfer of momentum from the application of local force by electromagnetic radiation
![[4 Physics/Images/radiation pressure.png|300]]

---
### radiation pressure absorption formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
p_{\text{rad}}=\frac{F_{\perp}}{A}=\frac{I}{c}\\
F=\text{force}\\
A=\text{area}\\
I=\text{intensity}\\
c=3\times10^{8}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### radiation pressure reflection formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
p_{\text{rad}}=\frac{F_{\perp}}{A}=\frac{2I}{c}\\
F=\text{force}\\
A=\text{area}\\
I=\text{intensity}\\
c=3\times10^{8}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### standing wave
- two identical waves traveling opposite directions that oscillate between constructive interference and destructive interference
![[4 Physics/Images/standing wave.gif]]

---
### wave position
- standing field magnitude as function of space and time

---
### wave position formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec E(x,t)=-2E_{0}\sin(kx)\sin(\omega t)\ \hat j\\
\vec B(x,t)=-2B_{0}\cos(kx)\cos(\omega t)\ \hat k\\
E=\text{electric field}\\
B=\text{magnetic field}\\
k=\text{wave number}\\
x=\text{position}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### resonant wavelength
- wavelength of normal mode

---
### resonant wavelength formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\lambda_{n}=\frac{2L}{n}\\
L=\text{length}\\
n=\text{harmonic number}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### resonant frequency
- integer multiple of fundamental frequency

---
### resonant frequency formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
f_{n}=\frac{nv}{2L}\\
n=\text{harmonic number}\\
v=\text{wave velocity}\\
L=\text{length}
\end{lgathered}
$$
