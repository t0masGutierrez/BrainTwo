### momentum
- difficulty of stopping or changing direction of motion
![[4 Physics/Images/momentum.png]]

---
### momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec p=m\vec v\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons second law of momentum
- net force equal rate of change of momentum

---
### newtons second law of momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=\frac{d\vec p}{dt}=
\begin{cases}
F_{x}=m\frac{dv_{x}}{dt}\\
F_{y}=m\frac{dv_{y}}{dt}
\end{cases}\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}\\
t=\text{time}\\
F=\text{force}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### impulse
- change of momentum equal sum of net force over time
![[4 Physics/Images/impulse.png|300]]

---
### impulse formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec J=\Delta\vec p=\int\vec F_{\text{net}}\cdot dt\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### impulse versus work
- net force dependent time equal impulse
- net force dependent position equal work

---
### impulse versus work formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec J(t)=\Delta\vec p\\
W_{\text{net}}(\vec r)=\Delta K\\
\vec J=\text{impulse}\\
t=\text{time}\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}\\
W=\text{work}\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
K=\text{kinetic energy}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### conservation of momentum
- if zero net force then zero change of momentum

---
### conservation of momentum formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{net}}=0\implies\vec J=0\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### collision
- short interaction between objects where they exert forces and exchange momentum

---
### collision formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{1}\rightarrow\leftarrow m_{2}\implies\vec J=0\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec J=\text{impulse}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### explosion
- increased kinetic energy
- momentum conserved
![[4 Physics/Images/explosion.png|400]]

---
### explosion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v_{1f}=\frac{-m_{2}}{m_{1}}\vec v_{2f}\\
\vec v_{2f}=\frac{-m_{1}}{m_{2}}\vec v_{1f}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### elastic collision
- kinetic energy conserved
- momentum conserved
![[4 Physics/Images/elastic collision.png]]

---
### elastic collision formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v_{1f}=\frac{m_{1}-m_{2}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\vec v_{1i}+\frac{2m_{2}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\vec v_{2i}\\
\vec v_{2f}=\frac{2m_{1}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\vec v_{1i}+\frac{m_{2}-m_{1}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\vec v_{2i}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### inelastic collision
- decreased kinetic energy
- momentum conserved
![[4 Physics/Images/inelastic collision.png]]

---
### inelastic collision formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_1\vec v_{1i}+m_2\vec v_{2i}=m_1\vec v_{1f}+m_2\vec v_{2f}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### completely inelastic collision
- decreased kinetic energy
- momentum conserved
![[4 Physics/Images/completely inelastic collision.png]]

---
### complete inelastic collision formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v_{f}=\frac{m_{1}\vec v_{1i}+m_{2}\vec v_{2i}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### center of mass
- point inside system of masses acting as the location of system mass
- or the mass-weighted average position of system mass
![[4 Physics/Images/center of mass.png|400]]

---
### center of mass formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec r_{\text{cm}}=\frac{\sum_{n}m_{n}\vec r_{n}}{\sum_{n}m_n}=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{n}m_{n}\vec r_{n}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
M=\text{system mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### center of mass velocity
- center of mass velocity dependent mass and momentum acting on mass

---
### center of mass velocity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v_{\text{cm}}=\frac{\sum_{n}m_{n}\vec v_{n}}{\sum_{n}m_n}=\frac{1}{M}\sum_n\vec p_n\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
\vec p=\text{momentum}\\
M=\text{system mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### center of mass acceleration
- center of mass acceleration dependent mass and net force acting on mass

---
### center of mass acceleration formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec a_{\text{cm}}=\frac{\sum_{n}m_{n}\vec a_{n}}{\sum_{n}m_n}=\frac{1}{M}\sum_n\vec F_n\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\vec a=\text{acceleration}\\
M=\text{system mass}\\
\vec F=\text{force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### rocket
- variable mass moving through time
![[4 Physics/Images/rocket.png|400]]

---
### rocket formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{\text{thrust}}=\vec v_{\text{ex}}\frac{dm}{dt}\\
\Delta\vec v=\vec v_{\text{ex}}\ln(\frac{m_{i}}{m_{f}})\\
\vec v=\text{velocity}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
t=\text{time}
\end{aligned}
$$
