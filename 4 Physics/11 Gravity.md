### point mass
- total mass concentrated at single point
![[4 Physics/Images/point mass.png|250]]

---
### point mass formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(\vec r)=m\delta(\vec r-\vec r_0)\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
\delta=\text{dirac}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons law of gravitation
- attractive force between two point masses directly proportional product of masses and inversely proportional square of distance between center of masses
![[4 Physics/Images/newtons law of gravitation.png|500]]

---
### newtons law of gravitation formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
r>R\implies\vec F_{g}=-G\frac{Mm}{r^{2}}\hat r\\
r\le R\implies\vec F_{g}=-G\frac{Mm}{R^{3}}\vec r\\
r=\text{distance}\\
R=\text{radius}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
m=\text{test mass}\\
\hat r=\text{direction}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### gravitational field
- region of space around mass where other masses experience gravitational force
![[4 Physics/Images/gravitational field.png|200]]

---
### gravitational field formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec g=-G\frac{M}{r^{2}}\hat r=\frac{\vec F_{g}}{m}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
r=\text{distance}\\
\hat r=\text{direction}\\
\vec F=\text{force}\\
m=\text{test mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### gravitational potential energy
- gravitational energy of position
![[4 Physics/Images/gravitational potential energy.png|300]]

---
### gravitational potential energy formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
r>R\implies U_{g}=-G\frac{Mm}{r}\\
r\le R\implies U_{g}=-G\frac{Mm}{2R^3}(3R^2-r^2)\\
r=\text{distance}\\
R=\text{radius}\\
U=\text{potential energy}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
m=\text{test mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### orbital motion
- if positive mechanical energy then hyperbolic orbit
- if negative mechanical energy then elliptical orbit
- if zero mechanical energy then parabolic orbit
![[4 Physics/Images/orbital motion.png|300]]

---
### orbital motion formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v<v_{C}\implies0<\epsilon<1\\
v=v_{C}\implies\epsilon=0\\
v_{C}<v<v_{E}\implies0<\epsilon<1\\
v=v_{E}\implies\epsilon=1\\
v>v_{E}\implies\epsilon>1
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### circular orbit
- uniform circular motion of object about mass
![250](4%20Physics/Images/circular%20orbit.png)

---
### circular orbit formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
E=-G\frac{Mm}{2r}=\frac{U}{2}=-K\\
\|\vec F_{g}\|=G\frac{Mm}{r^{2}}=\frac{mv^{2}}{r}\\
\|\vec v\|=\sqrt{G\frac{M}{r}}\\
\|\vec a\|=G\frac{M}{r^{2}}\\
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^{3}}{GM}}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
m=\text{test mass}\\
r=\text{distance}\\
U=\text{potential energy}\\
K=\text{kinetic energy}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### escape velocity
- minimum velocity for overcoming gravitational field

---
### escape velocity formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{E}=\sqrt{G\frac{2M}{r}}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
r=\text{distance}
\end{aligned}
$$

### conic section
- circle
- ellipse
- parabola
- hyperbola
![[4 Physics/Images/conic section.png]]

---
### conic section formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}\\
\frac{(x-h)^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{(y-k)^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\\
(x-h)^{2}=4p(y-k)\\
\frac{(x-h)^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{(y-k)^{2}}{b^{2}}=1
\end{aligned}
$$

### keplers 1st law
- planets orbit elliptically with the sun at one focus of ellipse
![300](4%20Physics/Images/keplers%201st%20law.png)

---
### keplers 1st law formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
r=\frac{a(1-\epsilon^{2})}{1+\epsilon\cos\theta}\\
a=\text{semi-major axis}\\
\epsilon=\text{eccentricity}\\
\theta=\text{angular position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### keplers 2nd law
- if planets nearer sun then faster motion and if planets further sun then slower motion
![](4%20Physics/Images/keplers%202nd%20law.png)

---
### keplers 2nd law formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dA}{dt}=\frac{L}{2m}\\
A=\text{area}\\
t=\text{time}\\
L=\text{angular momentum}\\
m=\text{mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### keplers 3rd law
- planets with larger orbits experience larger periods

---
### keplers 3rd law formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
T^{2}=\frac{4\pi^{2}a^{3}}{G(M+m)}\\
T=\text{period}\\
a=\text{semi-major axis}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}\\
m=\text{test mass}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### black hole
- object whose escape velocity exceed the speed of light
![250](4%20Physics/Images/black%20hole.png)

---
### black hole formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{E}\ge c\implies R_{S}=\frac{2GM}{c^{2}}\\
v_{E}=\text{escape velocity}\\
c=3.0\times10^{8}\\
R=\text{radius}\\
G=6.674\times10^{-11}\\
M=\text{source mass}
\end{aligned}
$$
