### scalar
- quantity with magnitude

---
### vector
- quantity with both magnitude and direction

---
### n-vector
- ordered n-tuple of real numbers

---
### n-vector formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec x=[x_{1},...,x_{n}]\\
x=\text{coordinate}\\
n=\text{number of coordinates}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### zero n-vector
- ordered n-tuple of zeros

---
### zero n-vector formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec0=[0,...,0]
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### magnitude
- nonnegative distance from the initial point to the terminal point

---
### magnitude formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\|\vec x\|=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+...+x_{n}^{2}}\\
x,y=\text{coordinate}\\
n=\text{number of coordinates}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### unit vector
- vector whose magnitude equal 1

---
### unit vector formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\hat x=\frac{\vec x}{\|\vec x\|}\\
\vec x=\text{vector}\\
\|\vec x\|=\text{magnitude}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### standard unit vector
- vector along coordinate axes whose magnitude equal 1

---
### standard unit vector formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec e_{1}=[1,0,0,...,0]\\
\vec e_{2}=[0,1,0,...,0]\\
\vec e_{n}=[0,0,0,...,1]
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### scalar multiplication
- scalar quantity multiplication with vector

---
### scalar multiplication formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
c\vec{x}=[cx_{1},...,cx_{n}]\\
c=\text{scalar}\\
x=\text{coordinate}\\
n=\text{number of coordinates}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### vector addition
- vector coordinate addition with corresponding vector coordinate

---
### vector addition formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec x+\vec y=[x_{1}+y_{1},...,x_{n}+y_{n}]\\
x,y=\text{coordinate}\\
n=\text{number of coordinates}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### linear combination
- sum of scalar multiplication with vector

---
### linear combination formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec y=\sum_{i=1}^{k}c_{i}\vec x_{i}\\
c=\text{scalar}\\
\vec x=\text{vector}\\
k=\text{number of vectors}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### component
- horizontal change equal first component
- vertical change equal second component

---
### scalar component formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
x_{1}=\|\vec x\|\cos(\theta)\\
x_{2}=\|\vec x\|\sin(\theta)\\
\|\vec x\|=\text{magnitude}\\
\theta=\text{direction}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### vector component formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec{x}=x_{1}\vec e_{1}+x_{2}\vec e_{2}\\
x=\text{coordinate}\\
\vec e=\text{standard unit vector}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### scalar multiplication property
- increase vector magnitude
- decrease vector magnitude
- negate vector magnitude
- absolute vector magnitude

---
### scalar multiplication property formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
c>1\implies(+\Delta)\|\vec x\|\\
0<c<1\implies(-\Delta)\|\vec x\|\\
c<0\implies(-)\|\vec x\|\\
|c|\implies\|c\vec x\|
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### scalar parallel property
- equal direction if and only if there exists scalar such that scalar multiplication equal vector

---
### scalar parallel property formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec x\parallel\vec y\iff\exists c:\vec x=c\vec y\\
\vec x,\vec y=\text{vector}\\
c=\text{scalar}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### scalar zero property
- for every product of zero there exists factor of zero

---
### scalar zero property formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
c\vec x=\vec0\implies(c=0)\lor(\vec x=\vec0)
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### vector equality property
- two vectors equal if and only if all corresponding coordinates equal

---
### vector equality property formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec x=\vec y\iff[x_{1},...,x_{n}]=[y_{1},...,y_{n}]\\
\vec x,\vec y=\text{vector}\\
x,y=\text{coordinate}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### vector arithmetic property
- commutative
- associative
- identity
- inverse
- distributive

---
### vector arithmetic property formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec x+\vec y=\vec y+\vec x\\
(\vec x+\vec y)+\vec z=\vec x+(\vec y+\vec z)\\
cd(\vec x)=c(d\vec x)\\
\vec x+\vec0=\vec x\\
1(\vec x)=\vec x\\
\vec x+(-\vec x)=\vec0\\
c(\vec x+\vec y)=c\vec x+c\vec y\\
(c+d)\vec x=c\vec x+d\vec x
\end{lgathered}
$$
