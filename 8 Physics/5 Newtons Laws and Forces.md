### dynamics
- study of how forces affect the motion of objects

---
### dynamics formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{A} \rightarrow \ ?

$$

---
### force
- vector quantity of push or pull on object
![300](8%20Physics/Images/force.png)

---
### force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F = F_{x}\hat i + F_{y}\hat j \\
F = \sqrt{F_{x}^2 + F_{y}^2} \\
\theta = \arctan(\frac{F_{y}}{F_{x}}) \\
F_{x} = F \cos (\theta) \\
F_{y} = F \sin (\theta) \\
F_{x} = \text{x scalar component} \\
F_{y} = \text{y scalar component} \\
\hat i = \text{x direction} \\
\hat j = \text{y direction} \\
\theta = \text{angle between vectors}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### net force
- vector sum of all forces acting on object
![300](8%20Physics/Images/net%20force.png)

---
### net force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{net} = \sum \vec F = (\sum F_{x}) \hat i + (\sum F_{y}) \hat j \\
\vec F = \text{force} \\
F_{x} = \text{x scalar component} \\
F_{y} = \text{y scalar component} \\
\hat i = \text{x direction} \\
\hat j = \text{y direction} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons first law
- object at rest remains at rest or moves with constant velocity unless acted on by nonzero net external force
![](8%20Physics/Images/newtons%20first%20law.png)

---
### newtons 1st formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{net} = 0 \implies \vec a = 0 \\
\vec F = \text{force} \\
\vec a = \text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons second law
- acceleration dependent mass and amount of net external force acting on mass
![](8%20Physics/Images/newtons%20second%20law.png)

---
### newtons 2nd formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{net} = m\vec a =
\begin{cases}
F_{x} = ma_{x} \\
F_{y} = ma_{y}
\end{cases} \\
m = \text{mass} \\
a = \text{acceleration} \\
F_{x} = \text{x scalar component} \\
F_{y} = \text{y scalar component}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### newtons third law
- for every action force there exists equal and opposite reaction force
- equal magnitude and opposite direction
![200](8%20Physics/Images/newtons%20third%20law.png)

---
### newtons 3rd formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{AB} = -\vec F_{BA} \\
\vec F = \text{force} \\
A, B = \text{object}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### normal force
- perpendicular force by surface on object
![300](8%20Physics/Images/normal%20force.png)

---
### normal force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{N} = m \vec a_{N} \\
m = \text{mass} \\
\vec a = \text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### friction force
- resistive force by surface on object
- static friction acts on stationary objects
- kinetic friction acts on sliding objects
![300](8%20Physics/Images/friction%20force.png)

---
### friction force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{s} \le \mu_{s} \vec F_{N} \\
f_{k} = \mu_{k} \vec F_{N} \\
\mu_{s} = \text{static friction coefficient} \\
\mu_{k} = \text{kinetic friction coefficient} \\
\vec F_{N} = \text{normal force}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### tension force
- pulling force by string on object
![300](8%20Physics/Images/tension%20force.png)

---
### tension force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{T} = m\vec a_{T} \\
m = \text{mass} \\
\vec a = \text{acceleration} 
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### weight force
- downward force by gravity on object
![300](8%20Physics/Images/weight%20force.png)

---
### weight force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec w = m \vec g \\
m = \text{mass} \\
\vec g = \text{gravity} 
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### centripetal force
- center seeking force causing uniform circular motion
![200](8%20Physics/Images/centripetal%20force.png)

---
### centripetal force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{c} = m \vec a_{rad}  \\
m = \text{mass} \\
\vec a = \text{acceleration}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### spring force
- restoring force by spring on object

---
### spring force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{s} = -k\vec x \\
k = \text{spring constant} \\
\vec x = \text{displacement}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### drag force
- resistive force by fluid on object

---
### drag force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{d} = -k\vec v \\
\vec F_{D} = \frac{-1}{2}\rho C_{D} A \vec v^2 \\
k = \text{linear drag coefficient} \\
\vec v = \text{velocity} \\
\rho = \text{density} \\
C = \text{drag coefficient} \\
A = \text{cross-section area}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### buoyant force
- upward force by fluid on submerged object

---
### buoyant force formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{B} = \rho Vg \\
\rho = \text{density} \\
V = \text{volume} \\
g = \text{gravity}
\end{aligned}
$$

### free body diagram
- draw body as the origin of the coordinate system
- sketch all forces acting on the body as vectors
- resolve all vectors into scalar components
- if equilibrium then newtons first law $\sum F = 0$ 
- if nonequilibrium then newtons second law $\sum F = ma$

### gravitational interaction
- responsible for the attraction between masses

---
### gravitational interaction formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{G} = G\frac{m_1m_{2}}{r^2}\hat r \\
G = \text{gravitational constant} \\
m = \text{mass} \\
r = \text{distance between centers} \\
\hat r = \text{direction}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### electromagnetic interaction
- responsible for the attraction/repulsion between electric charges

---
### electromagnetic interaction formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_{EM} = q(\vec E + \vec v \times \vec B) \\
q = \text{electric charge} \\
\vec E = \text{electric field} \\
\vec v = \text{velocity} \\
\vec B = \text{magnetic field}
\end{aligned}
$$
