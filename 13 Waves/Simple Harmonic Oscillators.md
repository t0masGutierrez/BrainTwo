\begin{aligned}
\psi(x,t)\\
x=\text{position}\\

\end{aligned}
$$

### simple harmonic oscillator
- periodic motion where object oscillate about equilibrium with restoring force directly proportional displacement

---
### simple harmonic oscillator formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
F+kx=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}+\frac{k}{m}x=0\\
x(t)=C_1\cos(t\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}})+C_2\sin(t\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}})=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\\
A=\sqrt{C_1^2+C_2^2}\\
\phi=\arctan({\frac{-C_2}{C_1}})\\
F=\text{force}\\
k=\text{spring constant}\\
x=\text{position}\\
t=\text{time}\\
m=\text{mass}\\
C=\text{constant}\\
A=\text{amplitude}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
\phi=\text{phase angle}
\end{aligned}
$$
