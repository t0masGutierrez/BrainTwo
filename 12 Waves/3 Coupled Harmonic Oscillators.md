### normal coordinates
- combinations of the physical coordinates that execute simple harmonic motion

---
### normal coordinates formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\vec Q=A^{-1}\vec x\\
&A=\text{eigenmatrix}\\
&\vec x=\text{position}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### normal mode
- amplitude pattern where all coordinates oscillate at the same frequency

---
### normal mode formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\vec x(t)=\sum_{k=1}^nA_k\vec a_k\cos(\omega_kt+\phi_k)\\
&A=\text{eigenmatrix}\\
&\vec a=\text{eigenvector}\\
&\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
&\phi=\text{phase angle}
\end{aligned}
$$
