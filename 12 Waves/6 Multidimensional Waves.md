### wavevector
- number of radians per wavelength

---
### wavevector formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\vec k=[k_x,k_y,k_z]=k\hat n\\
k=\text{wavenumber}\\
n=\text{normal}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### wavefront
- set of spatial points with constant phase at fixed time
![[4 Physics/Images/wavefront.png|400]]

---
### wavefront formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\omega t\mp\vec k\cdot\vec r+\phi=C\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}\\
\vec k=\text{wavevector}\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
\phi=\text{phase angle}\\
C=\text{constant}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### planar wave
- one-dimensional traveling wave whose surfaces of constant phase are parallel planes perpendicular the propagation direction

---
### planar wave formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\psi(\vec r,t)=A\cos(\omega t\mp\vec k\cdot\vec r+\phi)\\
A=\text{amplitude}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}\\
\vec k=\text{wavevector}\\
\vec r=\text{position}\\
\phi=\text{phase angle}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### planar wave equation
- temporal acceleration directly proportional planar curvature

---
### planar wave equation formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\frac{\partial^{2}\psi}{\partial t^{2}}=v^{2}\nabla^2\psi=v^2(\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial y^2}+\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial z^2})\\
\psi=\text{wave}\\
t=\text{time}\\
v=\text{velocity}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### cylindrical wave
- one-dimensional traveling wave whose surfaces of constant phase are coaxial cylinders perpendicular the radial direction

---
### cylindrical wave formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\psi(r,t)=\frac{A}{r^{1/2}}\cos(\omega t\mp kr+\phi)\\
r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\\
A=\text{amplitude}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}\\
k=\text{wavenumber}\\
r=\text{position}\\
\phi=\text{phase angle}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### cylindrical wave equation
- temporal acceleration directly proportional cylindrical curvature

---
### cylindrical wave equation formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\frac{\partial^{2}\psi}{\partial t^{2}}=v^2(\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial r^2}+r^{-1}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r})\\
\psi=\text{wave}\\
t=\text{time}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
r=\text{position}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### spherical wave
- one-dimensional traveling wave whose surfaces of constant phase are concentric spheres perpendicular the radial direction

---
### spherical wave formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\psi(r,t)=\frac{A}{r}\cos(\omega t\mp kr+\phi)\\
r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\\
A=\text{amplitude}\\
\omega=\text{angular frequency}\\
t=\text{time}\\
k=\text{wavenumber}\\
r=\text{position}\\
\phi=\text{phase angle}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### spherical wave equation
- temporal acceleration directly proportional spherical curvature

---
### spherical wave equation formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\frac{\partial^{2}\psi}{\partial t^{2}}=v^2(\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial r^2}+r^{-2}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r})\\
\psi=\text{wave}\\
t=\text{time}\\
v=\text{velocity}\\
r=\text{position}
\end{lgathered}
$$
