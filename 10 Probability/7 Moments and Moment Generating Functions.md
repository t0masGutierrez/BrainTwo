### moment
- numerical summary of random variable probability distribution

---
### moment formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mu_k'=E[X^k]\\
X=\text{random variable}
\end{array}
$$

---
### central moment
- numerical summary of random variable probability distribution about mean

---
### central moment formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mu_k=E[(X-\mu)^k]\\
X=\text{random variable}\\
\mu=\text{mean}
\end{array}
$$

---
### moment generating function
- function capable of generating moment

---
### moment generating function formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
M(t)=E[e^{tX}]\\
\mu_k'=M^{(k)}(0)\\
t=\text{independent variabe}\\
X=\text{random variable}
\end{array}
$$

---
### equal moment
- if equal moment generating function then equal random variable

---
### equal moment formula
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\forall t\in N_\epsilon(0):M_X(t)=M_Y(t)\implies X=Y\\
t=\text{independent variable}\\
N=\text{neighborhood}\\
X,Y=\text{random variable}
\end{array}
$$
