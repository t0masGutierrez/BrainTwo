### variable
- symbol representing quantity that can vary

---
### variable formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
x,y
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### constant
- symbol representing quantity that cannot vary

---
### constant formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
0,1
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### coefficient
- constant multiplying variable

---
### coefficient formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax\\
a=\text{coefficient}\\
x=\text{variable}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### algebraic expression
- mathematical statement composed of numbers, variables, and operations

---
### algebraic expression formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax+b\\
a=\text{coefficient}\\
x=\text{variable}\\
b=\text{constant}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### term
- piece of algebraic expression between addition or subtraction

---
### term formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax+by+c\implies ax,by,c\\
a,b=\text{coefficient}\\
x,y=\text{variable}\\
c=\text{constant}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### like term
- term(s) with the same variable and exponent

---
### like term formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax^{n}+bx^{n}=(a+b)x^{n}\\
a,b=\text{coefficient}\\
x=\text{variable}\\
n=\text{exponent}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### operation
- mathematical action performed on numbers or algebraic expressions

---
### operation formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+)=\text{addition}\\
(-)=\text{subtraction}\\
(\cdot)=\text{multiplication}\\
(\div)=\text{division}\\
(^{n})=\text{exponentiation}
\end{aligned}
$$

---
### order of operations
- PEMDAS and left-to-right

---
### order of operations formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{1. parenthesis}\\
\text{2. exponents}\\
\text{3. multiplication}\\
\text{4. division}\\
\text{5. addition}\\
\text{6. subtraction}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
