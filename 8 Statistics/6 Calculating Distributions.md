### normal
- probability as function of mean and standard deviation

---
### normal formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
X\sim N(x,\mu,\sigma)\implies P(X\le x)\\
X=\text{random variable}\\
N=\text{normal distribution}\\
x=\text{data}\\
\mu=\text{mean}\\
\sigma=\text{standard deviation}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### inverse normal
- value of random variable as function of cumulative probability

---
### inverse normal formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
x=N^{-1}(A,\mu,\sigma)\\
A=\text{area}\\
\mu=\text{mean}\\
\sigma=\text{standard deviation}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### z-score
- number of standard deviations from the mean

---
### z-score formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\\
z=\frac{\hat p-p}{\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}}\\
x=\text{data}\\
\mu=\text{mean}\\
\sigma=\text{standard deviation}\\
n=\text{sample size}\\
\hat p=\text{sample proportion}\\
p=\text{proportion}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### standard normal
- probability as function of z-score

---
### standard normal formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
Z\sim N(z,0,1)\implies P(Z\le z)\\
Z=\text{random variable}\\
N=\text{standard normal distribution}\\
z=\text{z-score}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### inverse standard normal
- z-score as function of cumulative probability

---
### inverse standard normal formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
z=N^{-1}(A,0,1)\\
A=\text{area}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### t-score
- number of standard errors from the mean

---
### t-score formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
t=\frac{\overline X-\mu}{s/\sqrt n}\\
\text{df}=n-1\\
\overline X=\text{sample mean}\\
\mu=\text{mean}\\
s=\text{sample standard deviation}\\
n=\text{sample size}\\
\text{df}=\text{degrees of freedom}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### t
- probability as function of t-score

---
### t formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
T\sim S(t,\text{df})\implies P(T\le t)\\
T=\text{random variable}\\
S=\text{t distribution}\\
t=\text{t-score}\\
\text{df}=\text{degrees of freedom}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### inverse t
- t-score as function of cumulative probability

---
### inverse t formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
t=S^{-1}(A,\text{df})\\
A=\text{area}\\
\text{df}=\text{degrees of freedom}
\end{lgathered}
$$

### chi-square-score
- ratio of variance

---
### chi-square-score formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\chi^{2}=\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2}\\
n=\text{sample size}\\
s=\text{sample standard deviation}\\
\sigma=\text{standard deviation}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### chi-square
- probability as function of $\chi^{2}$-score

---
### chi-square formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\mathcal X\sim\chi^{2}(x,\text{df})\implies P(\mathcal X\le x)\\
x=\text{$\chi^2$-score}\\
\text{df}=\text{degrees of freedom}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### inverse chi-square
- probability as function of cumulative probability

---
### inverse chi-square formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
x=\chi^{-2}(A,\text{df})\\
A=\text{area}\\
\text{df}=\text{degrees of freedom}
\end{lgathered}
$$
