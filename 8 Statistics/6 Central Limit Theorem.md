### sampling variability
- different samples from the same population have different sample statistics

---
### sampling variability formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\omega_{1},\omega_{2}\subset\Omega\not\implies\theta_{1}=\theta_{2}\\
\omega=\text{sample}\\
\Omega=\text{sample space}\\
\theta=\text{sample statistic}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### sampling distribution
- probability distribution of sample statistic across random samples of fixed size from the same population

---
### sampling distribution formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\theta\sim g(\omega_1,\omega_2,\dots,\omega_n)\\
\theta=\text{sample statistic}\\
\omega=\text{sample}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### law of large numbers
- sample mean approaches population mean as sample size approaches infinity

---
### law of large numbers formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\bar X=\mu\\
n=\text{sample size}\\
\bar X=\text{sample mean}\\
\mu=\text{mean}
\end{lgathered}
$$

---
### central limit theorem
- sampling distribution of mean approaches normal distribution as sample size approaches infinity regardless of population distribution

---
### central limit theorem formula
$$
\begin{lgathered}
\forall n\ge30:\bar X\approx N(\mu,\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n})\\
n=\text{sample size}\\
\bar X=\text{sample mean}\\
\mu=\text{mean}\\
\sigma=\text{standard deviation}
\end{lgathered}
$$
